# 🌿 ECO-BRAND & VISUAL GUIDE - ECOTECH EVOLUTION

**The Vibe:**
"The Future is Green." We are an environmental-first engineering company. The design should feel fresh, breathable, and powerful.

**Color Palette (The Eco-Shift):**
- **Deep Background:** #064E3B (Deep Emerald) or #0F172A (Deep Slate).
- **Primary Green:** #10B981 (Emerald Green - Trust & Energy).
- **Secondary Green:** #84CC16 (Lime Green - Innovation & Growth).
- **Soft Accents:** #ECFDF5 (Soft Mint for text backgrounds).
- **Text:** White (#FFFFFF) for readability on dark greens.

**Visual Elements:**
- **Icons:** Use organic line-art icons. `Leaf`, `Sun`, `Wind`, `Home`.
- **Backgrounds:** Use subtle gradients from Deep Green to Black.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use semi-transparent cards with a `backdrop-blur` effect to keep it feeling modern and light.

**Typography:**
- Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., **Geist** or **Inter**). Bold for headlines to show "Engineering Strength."

**Brand Voice:**
- Professional, Local (Struga-focused), and Sustainability-driven.
